## Still unresolved

- Exact champagne/sunburst/rose dial, gem-set bezel, caseback, crown, chapter ring and strap SKUs for this particular case. Engineering or visual colour selections are not supplier products.
- Which crystal/caseback/crown is included in each case package. Confirm contents before pricing separate purchases; do not infer an included part is free or double-buy it.
- Crystal seat/gasket/axial clearance and delivered NH05 holder/stem geometry. The pre-existing SKX-size crystals, chapter ring and rotating bezel prices are not fit approval for a 34mm NH05 case.
- Typography, date apertures, marker layouts and complication placeholders are engineering entries, not necessarily separately purchasable items. No independent price is fabricated.

The white dial and shorter luminous hands are explicit, manual-only budget alternatives. Selecting prices alone does NOT switch the Engineering/HD assets. Crystal alternatives replace the canonical crystal slot and hide competing crystal geometry. Physical locks, known mismatches and research-only records cannot be bypassed. Unknown fit is never promoted to verified. Engineering hand rendering now shares the HD model's selected lengths instead of fixed 10.2/13.6/14.2mm tips. Existing GLBs are reused; no new assets are generated.
