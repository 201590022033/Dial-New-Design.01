The specification pack contains appearance, casing, hand fitting, hand-setting
stem, 3H and 6H dial drawings, casing-ring geometry, assembly plan, and hands.
It is the controlling source for the movement interface. Marketplace listing
headlines are not acceptable substitutes for these dimensions.

## Platform boundary

The proposed Ladies Dress archetype uses an aftermarket 24.5 mm dial and a
nominal 34 mm case. Those are not TMI dimensions. They remain provisional until
an exact seller drawing or a measured golden sample supplies the case cavity,
dial seat, crystal seat, crown-tube centreline, lug width and gasket geometry.
The TMI drawing explicitly describes casing-ring retention; it must not be
treated as evidence that an arbitrary NH05 marketplace case is compatible.

The official NH05 drawing also shows a 19.00 mm reference dial. A 24.5 mm dial
may be used only when its NH05-specific feet/date aperture and the chosen case
dial seat are confirmed. The library therefore records 24.5 mm as a platform
choice, while preserving 19.00 mm in the manufacturer engineering reference.
