## Important costing rule

AliExpress item prices are not landed prices. Shipping is seller-, option-, and destination-specific and was not exposed by the independently indexed product pages used for this snapshot. A listing with unknown shipping must therefore remain **shipping pending**, never R0 shipping. Before purchase, capture the South-Africa checkout shipping charge and timestamp it separately.

SARS states that import VAT is 15% of the added-tax value, with a 10% customs-value uplift for goods imported from outside the customs union, plus any applicable duty. The indicative Rand figures below exclude shipping, customs duty, courier clearance fees, and VAT.

## Purchase-validation checklist

1. Select the exact variant, not the lowest teaser variant.
2. Set delivery country and postal code to South Africa.
3. Record item price, shipping, tax shown at checkout, seller, variant, and timestamp.
4. Confirm dimensions and interfaces: movement, stem position, dial diameter/feet, hand-hole sizes, crystal diameter/thickness, bezel insert OD/ID/profile, and chapter-ring OD/ID.
5. Prefer consolidated shipping only after comparing the combined checkout total; separate sellers can erase an apparent item-price saving.
6. Treat price snapshots older than 14 days as stale and re-check before ordering.
