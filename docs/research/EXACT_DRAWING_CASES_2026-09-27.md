## Evidence rule

The drawing and the price must resolve to the same product record. Similar-looking AliExpress cases, generic search photographs, and dimensions copied from an OEM watch do not qualify.

These drawings are sufficient for a **drawing-constrained visual GLB**. They are not manufacture-ready CAD because most omit hidden movement-holder, crown-tube, gasket, click-spring and tolerance details. A physical sample or supplier STEP/DXF/PDF section drawing is still required before labelling a GLB supplier-exact or suitable for fabrication.
