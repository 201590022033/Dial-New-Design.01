# Case-diameter reference photography research

Checked 10 October 2026. Two independent research agents investigated official small/large watch references. Model/diameter verification is recorded in the overarching plan. This manifest records candidates, not permission to republish them or proof of identity with the social screenshot.

## Integration decision

Use an original unbranded SVG schematic, labelled “Illustrative · not a product photograph”, and a link to the official reference page. No manufacturer photographs, social screenshot crops, hotlinked images, tracking pixels or new supplier records were added. Permission for dashboard redistribution has not been established. A product photo being publicly accessible is not treated as a reusable asset licence. Future licensed images require model verification, permission/provenance and visual inspection before integration.
